# Real-World Impact — PitchSync AI

## 🌍 Stakeholder Value Creation

### 1. International Fans
- **Multilingual Autonomy**: Instant assistance in 10 languages without needing staff translation.
- **Stress-Free Navigation**: Dynamic step-by-step route guidance to seats, restrooms, and food concessions.
- **Offline Ticket Protection**: Ticket details saved locally to survive cell tower congestion.

### 2. Disabled & Accessible Attendees
- **Dignified Stadium Access**: Automatic substitution of stairs for ramps and escalators for elevators.
- **Low-Sensory Routing**: Quiet corridor options for neurodivergent fans.

### 3. Stadium Operations & Security
- **Proactive Crowding Prevention**: Real-time SVG heatmap alerts staff before bottlenecks escalate.
- **Optimal Resource Deployment**: AI Auto-Balancer redistributes staff proportionally to crowd density.
- **Safe Rerouting**: 4-step Validation Wizard verifies that reroutes won't block accessibility or exceed shuttle capacity.
